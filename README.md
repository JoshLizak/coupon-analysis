# Findings Within coupons.csv
Overall, the most distributed coupons in the dataset were "Coffee House" (#1), "Restaurant(<20)" (#2), and "Carryout & Take away" (#3). Worth noting is that while the top three listed previously all have a similar volue of accepted coupons, the percentage of accepted coupons per type varies drastically with "Coffee House" having only ~50% acceptance while "Restaurant(<20)" and "Carryout & Take away" are both around 70% acceptance.

### This initial finding tells me that while coffee shops are far more prevelant in the dataset, cheaper food is far more appealing to the population.

Diving into the accepted coupons only, it appears that the largest demographic of people acceptiong coupons are younger people ages 21-31 and those ages 50plus. Of this demographic, most are driving alone or with friends and are either unemployed or are a student. Another key indicator here is that these people typically had no urgent destination during their drives, suggesting that they have plenty of free time to use these coupons.

The accepting demographic is also primarily single or married people which also ties into the age ranges previously discovered. 

### The inevitable conclusion is that those who are willing to accept a coupon are typically those with more free time on their hands which are most likely retired adults or college age students. And the primary coupon types they like are Coffee Houses and Cheap Food.
